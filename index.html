What the token does

Pirate Shop Access is a rare random find in Battle Royale chests. It unlocks Item Shop cosmetics that normally cost V-Bucks.

2 × 14

Days of free shop

Once in 2026, once in 2027. The tokens arrive as a pair.

Squad

Everyone gets it

If one player finds the token, the whole squad receives it. Play in fours.

Any chest

No secret spot

Regular, rare, Sprite, buried and Wanted Poster chests all count.

1 : 137,500

Odds are official

Epic confirmed it: 1 in 137,500 per chest. Treasure Hours boost it, so Saturdays are your best shot.

How to get it

Open as many chests as you can. Volume decides.

Start a match

Battle Royale on Crossbones Isle, 80 players.

Open chests

As many as you can. Volume decides.

Watch for the banner

The chest glows, then “YOU FOUND PIRATE SHOP ACCESS” appears.

Check the lobby

Both tokens show up. You keep them even if you get eliminated.

Activate

Open the Item Shop and pick “Plunder the Item Shop”. Wait for a good shop.

No glitches, no generators. Cannonball tricks at vaults are reported as an exploit and will likely be patched. Videos promising a “guaranteed token” and sites asking for your login are scams.

Questions

No V-Bucks. You only claim Item Shop cosmetics.
Do I get V-Bucks?

No. You only claim Item Shop cosmetics that normally cost V-Bucks. Gifting isn’t possible.

Do I need to win the match?

No. The token is yours as soon as it’s found.

Does a skin or location improve my chances?

According to Epic, everyone has the same chance. Locations with many chests only save time.

How long does the event run?

The weekly schedule runs until 5 December and the 2026 token is usable until 16 December, according to the tracker. Check in-game for changes.
